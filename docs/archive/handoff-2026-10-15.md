### Analysis findings (no new runs)

- k=1 misses at equal effective bpp (v3c): contiguous ~0–1%, random 4–10%, stride 4–30%. The model does not use periodic or payload structure.
- Scattered k=1 at 0.05 bpp is below the photo noise per cell (square-root law).
- Objective mismatch: the model trains on one label per tile. The map is a by-product, so clean cells are never trained to be cold. Per-cell training uses the generator's change mask (planned).
