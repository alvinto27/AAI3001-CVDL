## Scope and acceptance gates

- Keep RAISE in training. Add about **10,000** ALASKA#2 *various-size, uncompressed colour TIFF* masters, spread across camera models. Reserve about **eight complete camera models for test only**; take validation from the other models by photo. Do not use ALASKA JPEGs or the fixed-size TIFF variant for this experiment.
- Add about **3,000** released LSDIR HR PNG photographs as covers **without further resizing**, plus disjoint validation and test photographs. First check whether the accessible release actually contains the paper's full HR validation/test sets; if not, reserve seeded validation and test photos from the available HR train pool **in addition to** the 3,000 training photos. Do not claim an official LSDIR test result for a derived holdout.
- Remove DIV2K **from the proposed new training schedule only**. Keep its files, frozen manifests, cache and historical results. Tell teammates before changing defaults; preserve a legacy RAISE+DIV2K configuration and fixed DIV2K evaluation for regression. No source is silently deleted.
- Preserve the current 512-pixel tile grid, RGB8 clean-cover bytes, Mode 1 noise/embedding, paired labels and source provenance. No cover is in more than one split. Freeze the inventory, selection seed, camera aliases, hashes, source versions, and final counts before model training. Treat any change to the selection or decoder as a new version.
- Stage the experiment: complete full, verified Pi downloads after legal approval, freeze selections against the complete inventories, transfer only selected masters to PC/G4, then reader/manifest exactness gates, subset smoke and held-out evaluation. Do not make selection depend on download completion order. Scale **training/subset size**, not Pi acquisition, only if diversity helps and the user approves the cost. Compare against the **current best checkpoint on the same newly frozen evaluation sets**, not just old overall scores.

## Acquisition, legal checks, and reproducible selection

1. **Confirm terms before bulk access.** ALASKA's [project page](https://alaska.utt.fr/) states CC BY-NC-ND and forbids commercial use and redistribution of transformed material without authorization. Keep TIFFs and derivative covers/cache private, cite the source, and obtain a license decision before sharing outputs. The [LSDIR project repository](https://github.com/ofsoundof/LSDIR) points to a gated [Hugging Face release](https://huggingface.co/ofsoundof/LSDIR). A human must accept its terms and verify redistribution/derivative rights. The GitHub repository's code license does **not** establish an image license. Keep authentication out of Git, logs, and copied scripts. Stop if rights or access remain unclear.
2. **ALASKA inventory.** Download and version the public [number-to-camera index](https://alaska.utt.fr/ALASKAv2Base_by_DSC.txt); it describes about 80,005 IDs and roughly 50 camera groups. Parse explicit ID ranges, strip non-photographic calibration/texture groups if present, normalize names and variants into *camera-model* identities (not filenames or device numbers), and record each ID's camera and index revision. In particular, Nikon D90 also occurs in RAISE: do not call it an unseen-camera test unless D90 is excluded from every source's training data. Choose eight other model identities spanning phone and dedicated cameras, after checking counts and aliases; freeze their exact names. All photos of those models, including device aliases, go only to ALASKA test. After the full verified Pi download, use seeded, stable hashing of `(dataset version, camera, ID)` and documented per-camera quotas to choose about 10,000 across eligible models, including the eight test models. Target approximately 7,000 train / 1,000 val / 2,000 test **only if actual model sizes permit**; validation uses separate photos from training models. Freeze an ID-to-split table and actual per-model counts independently of transfer or extraction order. Do not tune quotas against test results.
3. **LSDIR inventory.** The [paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023W/NTIRE/papers/Li_LSDIR_A_Large_Scale_Dataset_for_Image_Restoration_CVPRW_2023_paper.pdf) reports 84,991 train, 1,000 validation, and 1,000 test images. The project `data/train.json` lists 84,991 HR training paths (`HR/train/00XX000/00YYYYY.png`). These released HR PNGs are already the result of shrinking the roughly 4K Flickr originals 4x with Lanczos before release; the X2/X3/X4 sets are additional lower-resolution versions, not input covers. Their average compressed size of about 1.8 MB suggests a long side around 2K pixels, **an unverified estimate**, not a measured dimension; measure and record actual HR widths/heights after authorized access. Repository issue #5 lists 17 HR train archives, `shard-00` through `shard-16`, each about 8.1–9.9 GB, roughly 155 GB in total; verify their recorded SHA-256 values **against the downloaded archives if filenames/content match**. Issues #3/#6/#7 report missing full HR evaluation data and an unavailable ETH host. The gated Hugging Face archive listing alone (including `val1.tar.gz`) does not establish that 1,000 HR validation and 1,000 HR test photos exist. Inspect authorized archive member lists and sample HR files before deciding. Prefer genuine, disjoint official HR val/test if verified; otherwise select at least 3,000 train plus explicit seeded val/test reserves from accessible train HR images, keeping the origin and derived-split flag in the manifest. No shared original or near-duplicate may cross splits.
4. **Complete-set selection, not shard-order selection.** Download and verify **all 17 HR train shards** first, rather than choosing an early shard merely because it arrived first. Index all 84,991 accessible HR train IDs and archive members, then sample 3,000 training photos plus any necessary val/test reserves across the entire verified inventory with a pinned seed and explicit duplicate-cluster exclusions. Shard order must not affect chosen IDs. A single shard has about 5,000 images and may bias camera/content/keyword/time coverage; spread selection across all shards and report actual per-shard counts. Keep the full verified archive set on Pi; only selected images and provenance move to PC/G4. Record revision, archive names and hashes, selection seed and all selected IDs. Do not scrape Flickr directly.
5. **Pi first, full resumable acquisition and private selection.** Use `/srv` on the Pi (860 GB free at planning time) for the **full** ALASKA various-size colour TIFF set (estimated 135 GB) and the **full** LSDIR HR train archive set (about 155 GB), plus available authorized HR val/test archives after verification. Budget at least about **290 GB** before extra evaluations, `.part` files, metadata and selected extraction; preflight actual sizes and retain a large free-space safety reserve. Keep verified full HR archives on Pi and extract only selected members to avoid a second full expanded copy. For each of the roughly 80,005 ALASKA TIFF IDs, keep a manifest of URL, ID, camera, size and hash; use politely rate-limited bounded connections, retries/backoff and `.part` files, resume only with validated HTTP range/ETag or restart safely, then verify file type/dimensions/hash and atomically rename. Do not mistake a partial/HTTP-error body for a valid image. For LSDIR use authorized authenticated archive transfers with resume support; verify **every matching HR shard** against the SHA-256 published in GitHub issue #5, check archive integrity, and reject absolute/`..`/symlink extraction paths. Check all expected IDs, archive counts and hashes before sampling. Record failures and byte counts to resume without silent skips. Keep the original archives and masters on the Pi; remove only failed `.part` scratch after safe retry, not verified full-set data. Transfer **only the frozen selected masters**, their original-file provenance and a hash inventory from Pi to PC/G4 with a resumable transport; verify destination hashes before building manifests/cache. The G4 is metered: start it only for an approved test and shut it down after delivery. Never commit images, credentials, archives, or caches.

**Pi ALASKA speed probe (performed for this plan):** Twelve files from the various-size colour TIFF endpoint, spread over numbered ranges, were valid TIFFs. They totalled **21,994,872 bytes**; serial wall time **57.852 s**, or **0.207 files/s and 0.380 decimal MB/s** including a 0.2 s courtesy pause per file. File sizes ranged **787,402–3,146,698 bytes**. At that observed serial rate, **80,005 files take roughly 4.5 days** by file rate; **135 GB / 0.380 MB/s takes roughly 4.1 days** by bytes. Allow roughly **4–5 days or more** for full ALASKA acquisition before retries/metadata/service changes; these are estimates, not a bulk-download promise. LSDIR transfer time is not measured and should be logged per shard. A thirteenth small TIFF checked with Pillow was RGB, 8 bits/channel, uncompressed, orientation 1 and carried a 748-byte ICC profile. Both Pi and local sample scratch files were deleted. Recheck service speed and rate limits before any large download; do not overwhelm the source.

## Readers, frozen records, and exactness

- Extend `LSB-Dataset-Generator/lsb_source.py` with an explicit TIFF RGB8 decoder for ALASKA. Validate a single frame, exactly three 8-bit channels, dimensions, TIFF orientation, alpha/palette absence and profile presence. Fix and record whether bytes mean *stored RGB samples* (recommended for reproducible steganalysis) or ICC colour-managed values; do not let different Pillow defaults silently convert them. The sample's ICC must not be mistaken for an instruction to transform pixels. Reject unsupported orientation/bit depth until a documented deterministic conversion and test exist. Keep contiguous uint8 RGB and reject images too small for a 512 tile. Read source type from manifest, not extension inference; do not alter existing NEF/PNG decoding.
- For LSDIR use the released **HR** PNGs as-is: decode to RGB8, check mode/profile/orientation and dimensions, and record the pixel/profile policy and decoder/Pillow version. Do **not** crop or resize the masters. The paper's 4x Lanczos downsampling and 48-pixel cropping describe upstream dataset construction, not steps to repeat; do not use X2/X3/X4 lower-resolution sets. Measure and record actual HR dimensions after access, then use only the existing tile grid to trim unused border pixels. Reject or explicitly record photos that yield zero 512-pixel tiles. Keep the original HR PNGs as immutable masters with member/shard and file hashes; decoded and cached RGB8 arrays must match pixel-for-pixel. Never resave covers in a way that changes their pixel bytes.
- Extend `scripts/build_manifests.py` and `data/<source>/` with new, separate versioned photo/split records. Use distinct `alaska` and `lsdir` photo IDs; keep the existing field contract (`photo_id`, `source`, `source_type`, `master_path`, `source_width`, `source_height`, `noise`, `camera_model`, `split`, master SHA-256). Add source-specific provenance separately: ALASKA numeric ID, original camera string, normalized model, TIFF ICC/orientation; LSDIR original HR member/shard and hashes, measured dimensions, decoder/profile policy and split origin (no local crop or resize). Use explicit `not_applicable` for LSDIR camera, not an invented camera identity. Pin seeds, complete candidate inventory, selection algorithm, excluded IDs and actual per-source/per-split counts in a record beside the new manifests. Audit disjoint IDs, file and decoded-pixel hashes before accepting either split. Never rewrite frozen RAISE/DIV2K CSVs.
- At 512 with the current `trim` grid, tile count per image is `floor(width/512) * floor(height/512)`; total pairs/epoch and fixed evaluation sizes depend on measured shapes, not just photo count. The sampled ALASKA TIFF dimensions 512–1024 yield between 1 and 4 tiles each, so **10,000–40,000 ALASKA tiles is only a bound** for images in that range. LSDIR HR dimensions and tile counts are pending inspection. Publish actual tile and clean/stego counts by split, camera and source, including rejected photos and unused grid-border pixels, before setting batch budgets. Repeat for 256 if that mode is used.
- Extend `lsb_cache.py` source-type/decode-version checks and `scripts/build_cache.py` inventory (currently hard-coded to 1,900 RAISE+DIV2K photos) for the new manifests. Store hashes of actual cover pixel bytes and master artifacts; a changed decoder/profile policy must invalidate cache entries. Keep strict no-decode-on-cache-miss behavior. Expand `scripts/check_cache.py` gates to verify all new masters and pixel hashes, with explicit memory/disk budgets rather than reusing the 1,900-photo expectation. Replay every tile and both Mode 1 labels from decoded versus cached RGB arrays on a small gate, then across the selected set, including fixed eval: tile coordinates, image/label bytes, pair IDs and order must match. Test multiple workers and epoch replay without changing old-source hashes.

## Training and evaluation design

- Add source names to `RunSettings`/`Corpus` and split-aware loaders in `lsb_torch.py` only after source records pass. Existing `source_weights` applies to **photo schedules**: it rounds each `(source,camera)` photo count and repeats or drops photos, rather than balancing tile counts. Repeats can replay the same tile/epoch payload, so do not claim independent new images. Compute actual training tiles after selection, audit the observed per-source **tile and batch** share for a fixed seed, and choose explicit weights/undersampling or revise the sampler if needed. As a starting comparison, frozen 512 training has 45,674 RAISE tiles and 8,276 DIV2K tiles (53,950 total); the proposed mix excludes the latter. Publish the new counts and weights, and run an unweighted control. Do not leak held-out cameras through balancing or source selection.
- Freeze a single held-out eval profile, payload/seed policy and pair IDs across experiments. Keep existing RAISE and DIV2K test evaluation unchanged as a regression panel; add ALASKA seen-model validation, ALASKA **unseen-model** test, and LSDIR test (official or labelled derived). Report clean/stego pair counts, accuracy, AUC and P_E per source, per ALASKA camera, phone versus dedicated camera, LSDIR origin, and an overall macro-source and micro-pair score. Report confidence intervals or at least uncertainty for small camera groups. Do not collapse the D90 cross-source overlap into an unseen-camera claim. Preserve self-contained source/camera fields in `test_predictions.csv` and notebook 02 analysis.
- Evaluate the **existing best v3c OTF checkpoint** without retraining on exactly these frozen new eval pairs and settings. Use a distinct run/output path and preserve old predictions/checkpoint. Then train a new RAISE+ALASKA+LSDIR model with the same architecture, tile, embedding and evaluation policy; log source weights, selected IDs, decoder/profile versions, dataset hashes, epoch schedule, worker settings and checkpoint. Compare both models on every old and new panel, not on different subsets. The earlier full G4 result (overall P_E about 0.0162; RAISE 0.010948, DIV2K 0.052524) is historical context only, not a new-source baseline. Run QUICK/CPU exactness before any approved full G4 run. Promote only if new-source held-out performance improves without an unacceptable old-source regression; report the actual trade-off, not just a pooled score.

## Risks and stop conditions

| Risk | Check / response |
| --- | --- |
| ALASKA CC BY-NC-ND and LSDIR access/derivative terms | Human confirms terms before bulk access, transformation or sharing; keep local outputs private and cite sources. |
| ALASKA server is slow or rate-limited | The 12-file Pi sample measured 0.380 MB/s with pauses. Use modest connections, backoff and a resumable inventory; stop on repeated HTTP errors. |
| LSDIR HR val/test unavailable; access is gated; full archives are large | Verify authorized files and member lists. Label derived holdouts honestly, budget all 17 verified HR train shards and preserve hashes. Stop if terms or archive integrity fail. |
| Camera or photo leakage and duplicated Flickr images | Normalize device aliases, avoid D90 as an unseen model, compare raw/derived hashes and perceptual duplicates within/across sources, and remove entire duplicate clusters from conflicting splits before freeze. |
| Colour/profile ambiguity or changed software | Freeze RGB pixel policy, decode versions and hashes; reject unexpected TIFF tags/PNG modes and invalidate cache on change. Never resize or crop LSDIR HR masters. |
| Train imbalance, tiny cameras, or selection bias | Select from the complete verified inventory, publish photo/tile shares, per-camera counts and shard coverage, and use fixed quotas/per-group metrics. |
| Memory, disk or G4 cost | Preflight full Pi archives/TIFFs, selected extraction, destination masters and cache separately; test only selected images on PC/G4 and do not launch a full paid run without approval. |
