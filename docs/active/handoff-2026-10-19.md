## 5. Rules learned today

- Decision rules: use paired-difference intervals or AUC. Do not use "model intervals do not overlap" or misses at one cut-off (confounded by score bias).
- Timing pilots: no per-step GPU synchronising callbacks (the arm A pilot under-measured speed by up to 2.5x). Use `pilot=true` and the warm epoch time; the real G4 epoch time is in `~/AAI3001-CVDL/runs-out/<job>/checkpoints/<job>/epoch_timing.csv` on the VM.
- SRM models on the PC: evaluate with `--set rocm_conv1_fix=off --set eval_batch_size=32`.
- G4: one run per boot; the Pi must be online before a paid boot; launch only from the clean launch worktree, dry run first.
